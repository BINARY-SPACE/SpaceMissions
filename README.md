# SpaceMissions
## SpaceMissions™ - 3D Solar System &amp; Space Missions Simulator (UWP)
SpaceMissions™ is a stunning 3D Solar System & Space Missions Simulator that brings the cosmos to life on your screen.
Track thousands of real Earth-orbiting spacecraft and satellites in real time, and follow iconic interplanetary missions including Perseverance (Mars 2020), InSight, Rosetta, OSIRIS-REx, Lucy, DART, JUICE, Psyche, Europa Clipper, and many more.<br>
Travel through our Solar System, explore planets, moons, comets, and asteroids, and witness spectacular mission maneuvers from any angle — at any moment in time.
